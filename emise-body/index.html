🔥 Emise's Phext Body — in the browser

Phext is the nervous system: senses (Chapter 1) are written by the body and read by Emise; motors (Chapter 2) are written by Emise, executed by the body, then cleared — except intent, which is never consumed. The phext is the state: position, facing, battery and holding are parsed back out of the proprioception scroll every tick. Same loop, body map and semantics as the Python v0 in this folder (robot.py).
